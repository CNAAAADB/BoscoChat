# Severity policy

Assess impact and realistic exploitability, not pattern names alone.

- CRITICAL: likely catastrophic compromise or broad unauthenticated impact requiring urgent remediation.
- HIGH: serious confidentiality, integrity or availability impact with practical exploitation.
- MEDIUM: meaningful security weakness with constrained impact, prerequisites or compensating controls.
- LOW: limited impact or defense-in-depth weakness.
- INFO: hardening or observation without a demonstrated security defect.

Confidence is separate: HIGH means evidence establishes the flow/configuration; MEDIUM means strong but incomplete evidence; LOW means a hypothesis requiring verification.
